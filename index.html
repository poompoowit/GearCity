💡 How Chassis & Gearbox Synergies Work in GearCity

In GearCity, a vehicle's overall appeal and market score are heavily influenced by the coherence between its Chassis, Engine, and Gearbox. Vehicle buyers have strict expectations based on their socioeconomic class and intended usage:

🏗️ Frame vs. Body Rigidity
• Ladder Frame (1902): Highest payload and rough-road durability; essential for Trucks, Heavy SUVs, and Commercial Vans.
• Unibody (1930): Lighter, cheaper to produce, and provides superior passenger cabin safety for Sedans, Coupes, and Hatchbacks.
• Spaceframe / Superleggera (1924): Maximum torsional stiffness at minimum weight; ideal for Sports Cars and Supercars.
🌀 Suspension vs. Handling & Comfort
• Leaf Springs: Heavy load capacity and rock-solid durability at the expense of ride comfort.
• Double Wishbone (1924): Superior camber stability and cornering control for sports vehicles.
• MacPherson Strut (1939): Compact and affordable; the foundation for economy compacts.
• Hydropneumatic / Adaptive (1944/1990): Isolates road bumps for top-tier Luxury Sedans and Limousines.
⚙️ Gearbox vs. Driving Dynamics
• Manual (Synchromesh 1912): High mechanical efficiency, low cost, and driver control for sports & economy.
• Automatic (1925/1950): Smooth torque-converter shifting preferred by luxury & high-wealth buyers.
• Dual-Clutch / DCT (1980): Ultra-fast sub-100ms gear shifts without power interruption for performance sports cars.

📖 How to Import XML Blueprints In-Game

1

Design & Download Blueprint

Use the Engine Optimizer tab to find your optimal engine, then click "Download Blueprint (.xml)".

2

Place the XML file in your GearCity Designs folder

Copy the downloaded XML file to the path for your OS:

🪟 Windows: Documents\My Games\GearCity\Designs\Engines\
🎮 Steam Deck / Linux: ~/.local/share/GearCity/Designs/Engines/
🍏 macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/GearCity/Designs/Engines/
3

Load Directly In-Game!

Launch GearCity $\rightarrow$ open the Engine Designer $\rightarrow$ click Load Blueprint. Your engineered engine is ready to build immediately with all sliders set perfectly!
